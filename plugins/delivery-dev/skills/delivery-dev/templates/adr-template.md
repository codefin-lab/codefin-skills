# ADR-<n>: <the decision, as a statement>

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-<n>
- **Date**: <yyyy-mm-dd>
- **Deciders**: <who>
- **Relates to**: <US-<epic>.<n>, a defect record, or another ADR>

## Context

What was going on that forced a decision. The constraints that were real at the time - a
deadline, a customer requirement, something the platform already does. Write enough that someone
who was not there can tell whether the constraints still hold.

## Decision

What we are doing, in the present tense. One or two sentences.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. This is the part that stops the question being
reopened every few months, so name the options that were genuinely tempting.

## Consequences

What this costs, what it makes harder, what has to change elsewhere. Include the unpleasant
parts - an ADR that lists only benefits is advertising.
