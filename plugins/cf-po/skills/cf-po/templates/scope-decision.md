# Scope decisions - <product or project>

One row per decision. Written at the time it is made, because the reason is what makes it
possible to revisit later, and the reason is exactly what nobody remembers.

| # | What | In / Out / Deferred | Why | Decided by | Date | Revisit when |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Paper statements | Out | every segment we serve is on the app; volume does not justify the print pipeline | | | a segment without the app is onboarded |
| 2 | Bulk export | Deferred | operations can run it by hand for the first month at about an hour a week | | | over two hours a week, or a second team needs it |
| 3 | Closed accounts | Out | retention is handled by the archive system already | | | the archive is retired |

**Deferred is not Out.** Deferred means it is expected and not now; Out means it is not coming.
Blurring them is why a backlog stops being believed - everything sits in a middle state nobody
plans around.

**Every row needs its reason.** Without it the item gets re-argued every cycle by somebody who
assumes nobody thought of it, and you cannot tell whether the reason still holds.
